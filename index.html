02 / CLEARING THE NOISE

Myth vs Fact

There’s a lot of skincare advice online. Not all of it is accurate — and not all of it is written with skin of color in mind.

Fact: Skin of color can still experience sun damage, worsening hyperpigmentation, and even skin cancer. While melanin offers some natural protection, it does not fully prevent UV damage. Daily sunscreen is still an important part of protecting skin health.

Fact: Acne and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation are related, but they are not the same thing. Acne refers to active breakouts, while the dark marks left behind are often a separate issue caused by inflammation. In skin of color, both problems may need to be addressed.

Fact: “Natural” does not automatically mean gentle, effective, or safe. Some natural oils, fragrances, and plant extracts can irritate skin, trigger eczema flares, or worsen breakouts and discoloration. What matters is whether an ingredient is appropriate for the concern and skin type.
